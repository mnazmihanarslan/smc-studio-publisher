# Privacy Policy — SMC Studio Publisher

Last updated: 19 November 2026

SMC Studio Publisher is a private desktop tool. It is used only by its owner to upload and schedule the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public.

## What data it accesses

- **YouTube (YouTube Data API v3)**: when a channel is connected, the app reads basic channel information (channel ID and title), uploads videos, sets their privacy status and scheduled publish time, and may post a comment on our own videos. Scopes: `youtube.upload`, `youtube.readonly`, `youtube.force-ssl`.
- **Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login)**: publishes Reels to the owner's own professional accounts and reads the account ID and username.

## Storage and sharing

- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
- Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram to perform the actions above.
- No analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data with anyone.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions> (Google) or in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens.

## YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
